# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome people with different levels of experience.
Critique ideas and code rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, demean, discriminate against,
or publish another person's private information. Keep technical discussions relevant to the
project and assume good faith while addressing concrete behaviour.

Examples of welcome participation include useful bug reports, reproducible tests, patient setup
help, thoughtful review and clear documentation. Spam, sexualised content, deliberate disruption,
personal attacks and sustained hostility are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues or other participation that violates
these standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately where practical and focused on community safety.

For non-sensitive concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository. For a sensitive report,
use the maintainer's current private contact option listed on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/newtonlorenz). Do not disclose another person's private data in
a public issue.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
